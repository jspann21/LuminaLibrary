## 2023-09-13 - Early Aggregation for Unpaginated Scans
**Learning:** In SQLite, when performing large unpaginated full-table scans (like dedup sweeps or unconstrained queries), correlated scalar subqueries in the `SELECT` clause (e.g. `(SELECT COUNT(*) FROM... WHERE book_id = b.id)`) are evaluated N times, causing an O(N^2)-like execution pattern.
**Action:** When scanning the entire table without a `LIMIT` (and avoiding a `GROUP BY` on the main table), apply early aggregation: move the aggregation into a derived table/CTE (`SELECT book_id, COUNT(*) FROM ... GROUP BY book_id`) and `LEFT JOIN` it to the main query. Conversely, correlated scalar subqueries remain faster than building derived tables when joining onto a strictly limited/paginated CTE (Deferred Join Pagination) or returning single rows.
